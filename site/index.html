How the scoring works

  1. Monday night. Eni closed at €14.20. Kronos says Friday ends at €14.45, Timestral says €14.10, the naive guess says €14.20.
  2. Each night after. One more real close arrives. The forecast waits until all 5 are in.
  3. Friday night. Eni closes at €13.95. Each forecast gets two scores. Average miss: how far off it was across the 5 days. Direction: whether it called up or down right by day 5.
  4. Beat the naive guess. How often a model missed by less than "nothing changes" did, same night, same asset. Daily prices are close to random, so this is the bar that matters.

Live and warm-up

Live counts forecasts made from launch night on. Each one is committed to the public repository before the real price exists, so it cannot be edited afterwards. Warm-up replays the 20 trading days before launch so the page has something to show from day one. The models may have seen those days during training, so read it as a preview.
